Scripture 365 Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

Scripture 365 is designed to work without creating an account. Reading history, saved verses, reminder preferences, theme preferences, reading streaks and Grace points are stored locally on your device.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes purchases and payment information. Scripture 365 does not receive or store your card or bank details. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app-user identifier, product identifiers, purchase receipts, entitlement status and limited device/store information to validate purchases, restore access and prevent fraud.

Images and external links

Wallpaper credits and original-image links may open Wikimedia Commons or another credited source in your browser. Those services apply their own privacy policies.

Sharing

Sharing is initiated by you through the device share sheet. Scripture 365 does not receive the destination or recipient.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases remain subject to parental controls, Ask to Buy and store account settings.

Retention and deletion

Local app data can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. Store transaction records are retained by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as required for purchase validation, accounting, fraud prevention and law.

Contact

Questions can be filed at the Scripture 365 support page.
